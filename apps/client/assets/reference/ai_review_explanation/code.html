A uniform flat circular disc of mass M and radius R rotates freely about a vertical frictionless axis passing through its center with angular speed ω₀. A small particle of identical mass m = M/2 gently drops vertically onto the rim of the disc and immediately sticks to it.

Calculate the final angular velocity of the compound system:

ω_f = ?  [in terms of ω₀]
A
ω_f = (1/2) ω₀ CORRECT

ω_f = (2/3) ω₀ YOUR CHOICE

AI DETAILED SOLUTION [STEP-BY-STEP]

VERIFIED
01 CONSERVATION OF ANGULAR MOMENTUM

Step 1: Apply Conservation of Angular Momentum about the vertical rotation axis since no external torque acts on the system (τ_ext = 0).

Initial Angular Momentum
L_initial = I_disc · ω₀ = (½ M R²) · ω₀
Note: The particle falls vertically; hence its initial angular momentum about the disc’s vertical axis is zero prior to contact.
02 COMPOUND MOMENT OF INERTIA

Step 2: After the mass m lands on the rim, the new moment of inertia is:

Total Inertia Formula
I_total = ½ M R² + m R² = R² (M/2 + m)
Given m = M/2:
I_total = ½ M R² + (½ M) R² = M R²
03 SOLVE FINAL ANGULAR VELOCITY

Equate initial and final angular momentum: L_initial = L_final.

ω_f = L / I_total = (½ M R² ω₀) / (M R²)
ω_f = ½ ω₀  [Option A]
